What this adds beyond USDM

USDM describes a protocol so systems can exchange it. This model goes further: it executes. The same objects that describe the trial also recompute its design, generate and analyse data, check themselves, and trace every change to its outputs.

CapabilityUSDM 4This modelTry it
USDM stays the right format for exchange with regulators and vendors, and it covers the schedule of activities and timelines, which this prototype doesn't build. The idea is to keep USDM as the export and put an executable layer on top.

Analysis decisions the protocol leaves open

With only a protocol, many rules a SAP would normally fix are undecided. Each is listed with what the protocol says, the option this model currently uses, and the alternatives. Decisions marked drives code feed the derivations directly: change one and the censoring calculator, unit tests, dry run and spec checks update at once.

Together these form a draft SAP decision log. Mark each one decided once the study statistician signs it off.

Spec checks

Automated checks run on every load: the protocol's own arithmetic, how much of the analysis the protocol leaves undecided, E9(R1) conformance, randomized stress tests of the derivation rules, and cross-checks against the public registries.

Study design

Event-driven, parallel-group superiority trial of dapagliflozin vs placebo on top of standard HFrEF care. Follow-up ends for everyone at a common censoring date chosen once 844 primary events are expected.

Arms

ARM A
Dapagliflozin 10 mg PO once daily (5 mg tablet allowed for dose reduction)
ARM B
Matching placebo, once daily
Randomization
1:1 via IxRS, stratified by T2D status (established T2D or HbA1c ≥6.5%). Block design not stated
Caps
≥30% of patients in each T2D stratum; LVEF, NYHA and AF categories may be capped
